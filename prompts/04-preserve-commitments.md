# Preserve commitments in a rewrite

Supply the original and proposed rewrite. Use this before sending a customer message, meeting summary, project update, or request for approval. The assistant identifies changes; the sender remains responsible for accepting them.

## Copy and adapt

```text
Original: [text]
Proposed rewrite: [text]
Desired style: [clearer, shorter, more courteous, etc.]

Compare the commitments in the original and rewrite:
- who is responsible;
- what they agreed to do;
- deadline or duration;
- conditions and dependencies;
- approvals granted, requested, or still missing;
- estimates, uncertainties, refusals, and disagreements.

Flag each change and quote the affected wording. In particular,
identify conditions omitted, estimates turned into promises,
and approval implied where it was only requested.

Draft a clearer version preserving those elements. Do not
resolve an unresolved disagreement or add a commitment.
Leave any consequential ambiguity for me to decide.
Do not send the message.
```

## Check the result

Read the commitments in both versions yourself. “We can deliver by Friday if the other assignment is removed” should not become an unconditional Friday promise. Do not assume silence after a meeting means someone accepted the summarized obligation.
