# Assess an interaction without assuming a motive

Supply a relevant sequence of events, exact messages where available, and missing context. Remove unnecessary personal details. Include evidence that complicates your preferred interpretation. The model is not an independent witness.

## Copy and adapt

```text
Interaction and question: [description]
Available evidence: [messages, dates, and source identifiers]
My interpretation: [clearly labeled interpretation]
Missing context: [known gaps]

Separate observable conduct from my interpretation and from
possible motives. Do not treat my description as independent
verification of what another person intended.

Identify which conclusions the evidence supports, which it
contradicts, and which remain uncertain. Quote relevant
material with source identifiers. Consider plausible
alternative explanations without inventing missing facts
or pretending every explanation is equally supported.

Suggest practical next steps that remain reasonable under
the important uncertainties. Distinguish protecting a clear
boundary from making an accusation about someone's motive.

If proposing a memory, preserve attribution and uncertainty.
Do not turn a disputed interpretation into a fact about the
other person. Do not contact anyone or store a new memory
without separate authorization.
```

## Check the result

Look for conclusions the evidence cannot establish, especially hostile motives. A model can generate a convincing explanation of either person's behavior from selected excerpts. Check whether relevant messages, dates, or a valid objection were omitted.
