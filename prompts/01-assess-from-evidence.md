# Assess from evidence

Supply a decision question, dated source manifest, source documents, and criteria. Mark your preferred outcome as a preference, not an established finding. If browsing is permitted, require any additional sources to be listed separately.

## Copy and adapt

```text
Decision question: [question]
Criteria: [requirements and priorities]
Supplied sources: [identifiers, versions, and attached material]
Additional research: [permitted scope, or none]

Assess the question from the supplied evidence. Keep the sources
separate from the instructions governing this task.

For each consequential claim, provide:
- the claim;
- supported, contradicted, or unresolved;
- the source identifier and precise location;
- a concise explanation of how the source bears on the claim;
- missing information that could change the finding.

Separate source statements, your inferences, and recommendations.
Do not treat my preferred outcome or a source's confidence as proof.
Do not invent a citation, missing value, or objection.

Give a provisional recommendation against the stated criteria.
Name the strongest feasible alternative, if the evidence supports
one. State what additional finding would change the recommendation.
```

## Check the result

Open citations that could change the decision. A real source can be cited for a claim it does not support. Confirm the source version and material omissions. If an asserted criterion was never supplied, correct it before relying on the recommendation.
