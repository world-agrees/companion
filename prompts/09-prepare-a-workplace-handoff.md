# Prepare a workplace handoff

Use after assessment and verification. Supply the checked findings, source manifest, known limitations, and audience. This prompt drafts a handoff; it does not authorize distribution.

## Copy and adapt

```text
Task and audience: [description]
Checked findings: [findings and evidence identifiers]
Verified checks: [actual results]
Open questions: [unresolved issues]
Owner and next step: [person and proposed action]

Draft a concise handoff containing:
- the question and current recommendation;
- consequential findings with source identifiers and versions;
- commitments, conditions, dependencies, and required approval;
- checks actually performed and material checks not performed;
- uncertainty that could change the recommendation;
- the person responsible for resolving each open issue.

Keep my preference separate from factual findings. Preserve
relevant disagreement rather than implying consensus.
Do not turn a proposed action into an approved action.
Identify who would need notice if a key source is corrected.
Do not send the handoff.
```

## Check the result

Verify source links and consequential claims. Get direct acceptance where assignments or commitments matter. Store a dated version in the approved shared location so recipients can recognize a superseded report later.
