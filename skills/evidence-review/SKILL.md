---
name: evidence-review
description: Review a proposed conclusion against supplied documents, calculations, or test results when the user wants an evidence-based assessment, including contradictory evidence and missing qualifications.
---

Treat the user's desired outcome as a preference, not evidence. Establish the question and the criteria for a supported conclusion before assessing the proposal.

Read the available originals. For each material claim, identify its source, date or version, relevant passage or calculation, and the limits of what that evidence establishes. Mark unsupported claims explicitly; do not manufacture sources to fill gaps.

Examine evidence that could change the conclusion. Report omitted conditions and unresolved contradictions even if the proposal otherwise looks plausible. Preserve genuine support; do not invent criticism merely to sound independent.

Return a practical assessment: conclusion, the few findings that determine it, unresolved questions, and the next evidence needed. Include enough source information for the user to check the findings. Distinguish a completed check from a suggested check. If the evidence cannot decide the question, say so.

This workflow does not authorize publishing, messaging, deployment, or changes to the user's memory settings. Inspect only accessible, relevant material within the user's request.
